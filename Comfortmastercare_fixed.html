About Us

MACOKI Clinic and Maternity Home

Welcome to the MACOKI Digital Ecosystem, a connected digital platform created to bring healthcare, education, patient services, professional services, school learning, pharmacy and inventory management, and maternal & reproductive health information together in one convenient place.

MACOKI Clinic and Maternity Home is the healthcare foundation of this ecosystem — designed to provide accessible, compassionate, preventive, maternal, child, community and general healthcare, while using appropriate digital systems to improve communication, record management, appointment coordination, health education, and continuity of care.

Instead of requiring patients, students, professionals and parents to navigate many unrelated platforms, the MACOKI Master Hub provides one central gateway to the services that matter to them — «Everything you need, connected in one trusted place.»

Our Vision

To build a trusted, accessible and technology-enabled healthcare and education ecosystem that improves the quality, accessibility, efficiency and continuity of essential services for individuals, families and communities.

Our Mission

To provide compassionate, accessible and responsible healthcare and educational services while using appropriate technology to connect people with information, professionals and services — promoting preventive care, maternal and child health, health education, and responsible digital health management.

Compassion

Every person treated with dignity, empathy and respect.

Integrity

Honesty, transparency and accountability, always.

Accessibility

Useful services and information, easier to reach.

Privacy

Personal and health information handled with care.

Leadership

The people behind MACOKI

ACO
Chief Executive Officer

Mrs. Amarachukwu Comfort Okeanya

Mrs. Amarachukwu Comfort Okeanya is the Chief Executive Officer of MACOKI Clinic and Maternity Home. She provides leadership and strategic direction for the organization, championing a healthcare environment centered on compassionate service, professionalism, accountability and community wellbeing.

Under her leadership, MACOKI continues to strengthen patient-centered healthcare, maternal and child health services, community health initiatives, professional collaboration, digital healthcare delivery and quality improvement — carrying forward her vision of a facility that leads with both heart and standard.

OKM
Chairman

Mr. Okeanya Kingsley Madukaibeya

Mr. Okeanya Kingsley Madukaibeya is the Chairman of MACOKI — a Community Health Practitioner (CHP) and Public Health Practitioner (PHP) with hands-on experience across community and clinical health services, including maternal and child health, immunization, patient assessment, emergency and first-aid support, infection prevention and control, family planning, and healthcare administration and coordination.

He holds a Diploma in Community Health and is completing a B.Sc. in Public Health at the National Open University of Nigeria (NOUN), alongside training in Cybersecurity Fundamentals at Auxano Institute of Technology — a combination that shapes his hands-on interest in healthcare technology and digital service delivery, and his conviction that responsible digital systems belong in every community clinic.

As Chairman, he leads MACOKI's long-term strategic development and the integration of healthcare, education and technology into one connected ecosystem — building not just a facility, but a structured platform through which people can obtain knowledge, connect with professionals and access essential services, one login at a time.
